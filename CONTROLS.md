# Controls: Meta Quest 2

## Buttons

| Control | Action |
|---|---|
| **Grip** (middle-finger button) | Grab equipment. Hold it to keep the item in your hand and let go to drop it. Items snap into your hand at their handle, even if you grab them with the laser from a distance. |
| **Trigger** (index finger) | Use the item you are holding, for example calling 911 on the phone. Also presses menu buttons. |
| **Laser + Trigger** | Point at a menu button and pull the trigger. You can also touch buttons directly with the controller. |
| **Right thumbstick forward** | Aim, then release to teleport. |
| **Right thumbstick left / right** | Turn. Snap turn is the default; smooth turn can be chosen in Settings. |
| **Left thumbstick** | Smooth movement, when it is turned on in Settings. |
| **Menu button** (left controller) | Pause: resume, restart or return to the main menu. The clock stops while paused. |

## Hands-on actions in the scenarios

| Action | How to do it |
|---|---|
| Check the scene is safe | At the start, turn your head to look left and right. |
| Check / tap the person | Touch their shoulder with your controller. |
| Answer a decision card | Point and pull the trigger on an answer. The clock stops while the card is open. |
| Talk to someone | Use the "Say..." panel next to the person. |
| Call 911 | Pick up the phone (grip) and pull the trigger. The call is on speaker, so you can put the phone down. |
| Put on gloves | Grab the blue glove box and hold it for a moment. Your controllers turn blue. |
| Apply a dressing / blanket / bandage | Carry it to the right spot and hold it there for a moment, or let go. It snaps into place. A green outline shows where it goes. |
| Press firmly on a wound | Put your hand on the dressing and hold **grip or trigger**. Keep holding until the meter is full. Letting go early counts as a mistake. |
| Hold the infant | Grab the baby (grip). The baby lies along your forearm, **face-down when your palm faces down**. Tilt your hand forward so the **head is lower than the chest**. |
| Back blows | With your other hand, swing down firmly onto the baby's back between the shoulder blades. |
| Turn the infant face-up | Turn your holding hand over (palm up), keeping the head low. |
| Chest thrusts | Push your other hand into the centre of the baby's chest, then let it come back up. |
| Help a seated person lie down | Grab their shoulder (grip) and move your hand down. |
| Check breathing | Rest your hand on the person's chest for a few seconds. |

## Comfort tips

- The patients in two scenarios are on the ground. Kneel down, or use **Settings > Seated mode height** to raise your view.
- If smooth movement makes you uncomfortable, keep **Movement: Teleport only** and the **Comfort vignette** on. Both are the defaults.

## Desktop (no headset) testing in the Unity editor

Turn on **Tools > First Aid VR > Desktop Simulator (no headset)** and press Play. The XR Interaction Simulator shows an on-screen panel with its keyboard and mouse controls. Use them to move the head and each controller and to press grip and trigger.
